Add poses from photos

Drop in photos of great poses. The tool finds the body skeleton in each one, right here in your browser, and turns it into a pose for the coach. Only the skeleton is kept, never the photo.

Use photos you're allowed to use
  • Your own photos, or freely licensed ones from Unsplash, Pexels or Wikimedia Commons. Put the photo's page link in "Source" so it's credited.
  • Not photos saved from Instagram, Pinterest or other people's accounts: those are copyrighted and the people in them didn't agree.
  • Best results: one person, whole body in view, facing roughly toward the camera.
